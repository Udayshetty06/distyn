### Z wave

low power wireless communication protocol - for home automation and smart buildings - operate at sub-Ghz ISM band - minimizes interference from Bluetooth and wifi
standard : ITU-T G.9959
data rate: 9.6 kbps , 40 kbps , 100 kbps (z-wave plus)
range: 30-100 m indoors , 300 m outdoors (LOS)
topology : mesh
maximum nodes : 232 devices
security : AES-128 encryption with security S2 framework
AES-128 encryption was very good until introduction of quantum decrypting
limitation : only 232 devices supported

### DHT SENSOR

important point - 10k ohm pull up resistor - used to distinguish logic 0 or logic 1 in case of floating points

serial communication : data line is high initially host makes it low to signal the start and makes it high again and wait for the response 

sensor or slave makes the signal low and then high to send out response

then the actual data transfer happens : longer duration of high for 1 . lower duration of high for 0 

then sensor makes the line low again and hands the contol to master

dht 22

**1–10 ms**: MCU pulls the line **LOW** to initiate the start signal.

**20–40 µs**: MCU releases the line **HIGH** and waits for the sensor.

**80 µs**: Sensor pulls the line **LOW** to acknowledge presence.

**80 µs**: Sensor releases the line **HIGH** to prepare for data.

**50 µs**: Sensor pulls the line **LOW** to signal the start of **every** bit.

**26–28 µs**: Sensor holds the line **HIGH** to transmit a **Bit '0'**.

**70 µs**: Sensor holds the line **HIGH** to transmit a **Bit '1'**.

**50 µs**: Sensor pulls the line **LOW** one final time after the 40th bit to signal completion

#### FRAME FORMAT of dht11
